Empanada

Description

Empanadas are a type of pastry that is popular in many Latin American countries.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Ground beef
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Roll out the dough and cut into circles.
  3. Place a small amount of filling on one half of each circle.
  4. Fold the other half over the filling and crimp the edges.
  5. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown.
