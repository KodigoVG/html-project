Hallaca

Description

Hallaca is a traditional Venezuelan dish that is typically served during the Christmas season. It consists of a cornmeal dough filled with a mixture of meats, vegetables, and spices, wrapped in plantain leaves, and then boiled or steamed. The filling can include beef, pork, chicken, raisins, olives, and capers, among other ingredients. Hallacas are often enjoyed with family and friends as part of festive celebrations.
