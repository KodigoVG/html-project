Hallaca

Description

Hallaca is a traditional Venezuelan dish that is typically served during the Christmas season. It consists of a cornmeal dough filled with a mixture of meats, vegetables, and spices, wrapped in plantain leaves, and then boiled or steamed. The filling can include beef, pork, chicken, raisins, olives, and capers, among other ingredients. Hallacas are often enjoyed with family and friends as part of festive celebrations.

Ingredients

  • Cornmeal dough
  • Ground beef
  • Ground pork
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • raisins
  • olives
  • capers

Steps

  1. Prepare the cornmeal dough by mixing cornmeal, water, and a pinch of salt until it forms a smooth dough.
  2. In a separate pan, cook the ground beef and pork with onions, garlic, and tomato sauce until the meat is browned and the flavors are well combined.
  3. Add raisins, olives, and capers to the meat mixture and cook for a few more minutes.
  4. Take a portion of the cornmeal dough and flatten it into a disc. Place a spoonful of the meat mixture in the center of the disc and fold the dough over to enclose the filling, forming a half-moon shape. Repeat this process for the remaining dough and filling.
  5. Wrap each hallaca in a plantain leaf, securing it with kitchen twine or toothpicks.
  6. Boil or steam the wrapped hallacas for about 1 to 1.5 hours, until the dough is cooked through.
  7. Remove the hallacas from the heat and let them cool slightly before unwrapping and serving. Enjoy your hallacas with family and friends!
