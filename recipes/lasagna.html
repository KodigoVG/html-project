Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain any excess fat.
  4. Add the tomato sauce and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  5. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
